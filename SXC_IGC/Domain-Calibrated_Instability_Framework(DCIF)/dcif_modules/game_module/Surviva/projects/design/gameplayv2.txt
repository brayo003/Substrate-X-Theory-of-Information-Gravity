1. The Three Fields (strict separation)
T (Tension)
Fast variable
Driven by:
player actions
predictability (via P)
memory influence (via M)
Purpose:
→ controls when rupture happens
P (Prediction)
Derived variable (not independent)
Represents:
→ how well the substrate can predict the player
Mechanically:
→ increases when player behavior is repetitive / directional
Purpose:
→ accelerates T growth
→ makes rupture inevitable if player is predictable
M (Memory / Knowledge field)
Slow variable
Represents:
→ how much of the world the player has explored
Purpose:
→ unlocks rewards (artifacts)
→ also increases danger indirectly
2. Core Loop (this is the engine’s real job)

This is the invariant loop your system must satisfy:

Phase 1 — Exploration
Player moves → M increases
Substrate learns → P slowly increases
T rises gradually
Phase 2 — Opportunity
When M crosses thresholds:
→ artifacts can spawn

This is not deterministic, it’s probabilistic:

M in [30–40] → low-tier spawn chance
M in [40–50] → higher-tier spawn chance

So:

M does NOT directly give artifacts
it unlocks the possibility space

Phase 3 — Pressure
As player continues:
P increases (predictability)
T accelerates

Player now feels:

“something is coming”

Phase 4 — Decision window

Player must choose:

keep exploring (risk rupture)
or search for artifact (reward)

This is where gameplay lives.

Phase 5 — Rupture (punishment event)

Triggered when:

T high AND P high

Effects:

T resets
P collapses
M partially decays

3. Artifact = Checkpoint System (this is your key insight)

You correctly shifted M into a two-layer system:

Layer A — Raw M (volatile)

Layer B — Tier Progress (persistent)

Formal behavior:
