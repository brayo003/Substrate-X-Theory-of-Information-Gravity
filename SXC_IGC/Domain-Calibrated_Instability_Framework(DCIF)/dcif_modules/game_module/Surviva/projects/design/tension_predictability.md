The Corrected System: Low Tension Is Not Safe
The Core Inversion
What You Thought	What Is True
Low tension = safe	Low tension = predictable
High tension = dangerous	High tension = losing yourself
You play to survive	You play to avoid being known

The game does not reward safety. Safety is a pattern. Patterns are learned. Learning is captured.
The Two Axes
Axis	What It Measures
Tension (T_sys)	How much the system is responding. Your fear. Your activity.
Predictability (P_sys)	How well the system knows you. Your patterns. Your repetition.

These are independent. You can have:
State	Tension	Predictability	What Happens
Safe but Known	Low	High	Sanctuary degrades. Stalker waits. Trap builds.
Active but Free	High	Low	Stalker struggles. You survive. Chaos protects you.
Danger	High	High	Shatter. You become it.
Empty	Low	Low	Nothing. You are forgotten.
What Happens When You Stay Safe
Day	You Do	System Does
1	Stay in sanctuary	Nothing. Memory field begins to build.
2	Stay again	Predictability rises. Sanctuary flickers.
3	Stay again	Stalker circles outside. Sanctuary creaks.
4	Stay again	Stalker waits at threshold. Sanctuary unsafe.
5	Stay again	You cannot stay. The system has learned your hiding. You must leave.

You are not safe. You are predictable. Predictability is captured.
What Happens When You Act
You Do	System Does
Go to new places	Memory field spreads. Predictability drops.
Change paths	Stalker loses pattern. Tension stays manageable.
Be chaotic	Stalker struggles. You survive.
Be creative	The system does not know you. You are free.

Chaos is safety. Predictability is danger. You do not play to survive. You play to be unknown.
The Four Quadrants
	Low Predictability	High Predictability
Low Tension	Empty. Forgotten. Nothing happens.	Safe but Known. Sanctuary degrades. Stalker waits. Trap builds.
High Tension	Active but Free. Stalker struggles. You survive.	Shatter. You become it. Body trails. You are absorbed.

The only safe quadrant is Active but Free. The only dangerous quadrant is Safe but Known.

The Trap

You cannot hide. The system learns your hiding.
You cannot repeat. The system learns your patterns.
You cannot stay still. The system learns your stillness.

You must move. You must change. You must be unpredictable. Or the system will know you. And when it knows you, you become it.
The Final Horror

Not being hunted. Not being chased.
Being known. Being predictable. Being captured without moving. Being absorbed without fighting.
