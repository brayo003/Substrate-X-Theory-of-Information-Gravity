INTERPRETATION NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Precision = TP/(TP+FP): of all bars flagged, what fraction
   precede a transition? Higher is better.
2. Recall = TP/(TP+FN): of all transitions, what fraction were
   flagged? Higher is better.
3. F1 = harmonic mean of precision and recall.
4. FPR = FP/(FP+TN): false alarm rate.
5. A detector with no skill matches the base rate in precision
   and has F1 ≈ 2*base_rate/(1+base_rate).
6. |grad_ln_s| is the direct mathematical input to Q.
   If Q and |grad_ln_s| have identical F1, Q adds nothing
   beyond its own input for transition detection.
