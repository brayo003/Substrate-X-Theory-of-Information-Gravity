## 1. Justification of the Verdict

### 1.1 The Baseline Failure Across 20.5 Years of Untouched Data (2004–2024)
- When subjected to a rigorous, leak-free chronological walk-forward test across 20.5 years of previously unexamined historical data ($N = 269$ trades) using the `CorrectedEngine` under baseline $\$0.75$/oz round-trip costs:
  - **Net Profit Factor**: **1.073** (well below the pre-registered hurdle of $1.20$).
  - **Net Expectancy**: **+$1.32 USD/oz** (representing less than $0.05\%$ per trade on average price).
  - **95% Moving Block Bootstrap CI**: **[-$2.32, +$5.05] USD/oz**.
  - **Verdict Rationale**: Because the 95% block-bootstrap confidence interval comfortably encompasses zero and negative expectancy, the positive sample mean is statistically indistinguishable from random noise.
  - **Multi-Instrument Cross-Validation**:
    - **COMEX Gold Futures (`GC=F`, 2000–2024)**: $N=359$, Net PF = **1.010**, Net Exp = **+$0.15/oz**, Block CI = **[-$2.54, +$3.53]**.
    - **SPDR Gold ETF (`GLD`, 2004–2024)**: $N=287$, Net PF = **0.977**, Net Exp = **-$0.04/share**, Block CI = **[-$0.37, +$0.34]**.
    - Across all three independent instruments, the symmetric Donchian20 strategy is break-even or loss-making out-of-sample.

### 1.2 The Asymmetry: Short Leg is Chronic Capital Destruction
- Across every historical fold (2004–2016, 2017–2019, 2020–2022, 2023–2024, and 2025–2026):
  - **Short Trades ($N=110$)**: Win Rate = **29.1%**, Net PF = **0.643**, Net Expectancy = **-$7.82 USD/oz**, Total Loss = **-$859.78 USD**.
  - Short breakouts on gold are systematically penalized by gold's positive drift and right-skewed volatility shocks. A symmetric rule that takes short signals blindly destroys the profits generated by the long side.

### 1.3 Prior Report Deconstruction: Small-Sample Illusion and Bug Amplification
The preliminary promising results reported in `CORRECTED_DECISION_REPORT.md` (WF Net PF = $2.042$, Exp = $+\$56.53$, $N=21$) were driven by three confounding factors:
1. **Regime Bias**: The 2025–2026 evaluation period coincided with an unprecedented parabolic bull market (spot gold surged from $\$2,600$ to over $\$4,500$/oz). In that 18-month window, 13 long trades caught vertical trend extensions, generating 90% of the entire system's profits.
2. **Small-Sample Variance**: With only 21 trades, the prior report's own 95% bootstrap CI was $[-\$19.05, +\$131.57]$. The interval spanned zero from the start, as acknowledged in the caveats.
3. **Execution Bugs**:
   - ATR lookahead (`atr[i]` calculated from that day's high/low) leaked end-of-day volatility into morning stop/target calculations.
   - Overnight gap neglect filled stops at theoretical prices rather than market opens.
   - Entry-day immunity protected newly entered trades from day-1 stop outs.
   - Correcting these bugs reduced net expectancy on that same period by $\$1.72$/oz and revealed realistic drawdowns.
4. **The $55–$57.28 Cost-Tolerance Claim**:
   - The claim that *"PF stays above 1 until round-trip costs approach ~$55 per ounce"* was mathematically derived from naive linear profit deduction ($1,202.87 / 21 = \$57.28$/oz).
   - Our independent calculation proves that at $\$55$/oz cost, **7 winning trades migrate into losses**, collapsing the win rate to **28.6%** (6 wins, 15 losses). The strategy was never structurally cost-resilient; it simply held a few massive outlier winners from a once-in-a-generation bull run.

### 1.4 Substrate AI Transport Implementation Failure
Testing the real telegraph PDE scheme from `v12_validate/transport_layer.py`:
- **Transport Slope Gate**: Filters out 100 trades, lifting Net PF to **1.366**, but the 95% Block Bootstrap CI remains **[-$0.85, +$11.75]** (spanning negative values). More importantly, simply trading Long-Only without transport produces a superior Net PF of **1.478** with a strictly positive CI ([+$1.33, +$13.49]).
- **Transport Standalone Signal**: Trading transport divergence directly ($s - s_{\text{transport}} > 0.05$) results in catastrophic performance: $N=691$, Net PF = **0.668**, Net Exp = **-$6.70 USD/oz**, Total Loss = **-$4,630 USD**.
- **Non-Transport Ablation**: A standard 50-day EMA filter delivers Net PF = **1.064** with no mathematical transport machinery. Transport adds no demonstrable incremental value.

## 2. Strongest Supporting Evidence

1. **Massive Sample Size ($N=269$ on Spot, $N=359$ on Futures)**:  
   Moving from $N=21$ trades over 1.5 years to $N=269$ trades over 20.5 years eliminates small-sample regime bias.
2. **Three Independent Instruments Converge on Breakeven**:  
   Spot XAUUSD (Net PF 1.073), COMEX Futures (Net PF 1.010), and GLD ETF (Net PF 0.977) all converge to $\approx 1.0$, proving the result is an invariant property of the strategy, not an artifact of data cleaning or broker quotes.
3. **Serial Autocorrelation Penalizes the Strategy**:  
   Moving block bootstrap ($L=10$ bars) broadens the uncertainty interval to $[-\$2.32, +\$5.05]$, proving that returns cluster and cannot be assumed independent.
4. **Overnight Carry Drag**:  
   Adding realistic swap financing ($-3.0\%$ annualized on long gold) cuts net expectancy from $+\$1.32$ down to $+\$0.50$/oz (Net PF 1.027), leaving zero buffer for slippage or execution frictions.

## 3. Strongest Evidence Against the Verdict

1. **Long-Only Variant Demonstrates Positive Statistical Expectancy**:  
   If one restricts Donchian20 strictly to Long-Only ($N=166$ trades over 2004–2024):
   - Net PF = **1.478**
   - Net Expectancy = **+$7.53 USD/oz**
   - 95% Block Bootstrap CI = **[+$1.33, +$13.49] USD/oz** (strictly positive).
   - This could be interpreted by some as evidence of a "one-sided edge."
2. **Why Long-Only Is Still Not an Alpha Strategy**:  
   Passive Buy-and-Hold Gold returned **+580.9%** (+9.8% annualized) over the same period. Long-Only Donchian held gold only ~35% of the time, produced a fraction of the cumulative return (+\$1,250 USD total on 1 oz), suffered severe whipsaws during the 2011–2015 bear market, and incurs continuous transaction costs. Its positive expectancy is almost entirely an extraction of gold's underlying beta drift, not true timing alpha.
