# 5. Source and Sink Terms

Information can be created or destroyed through interactions with physical processes.

The source function is modeled as:

\[
S =
\alpha E
+
\beta \nabla\cdot(E v_{sub})
+
\gamma F
-
\sigma_{irr}
\]

---

## Direct Energy Coupling

\[
\alpha E
\]

Energy density may generate information through physical interactions.

α = coupling coefficient.

---

## Energy Flow Coupling

\[
\beta \nabla\cdot(E v_{sub})
\]

Transport of energy can produce information through dynamic processes.

---

## Force Coupling

\[
\gamma F
\]

Force density acting on the substrate may generate informational structure.

---

## Irreversible Loss

\[
\sigma_{irr}
\]

Irreversible entropy production removes information.

---

## Final Source Function

\[
S =
\alpha E
+
\beta \nabla\cdot(E v_{sub})
+
\gamma F
-
\sigma_{irr}
\]

This term links information dynamics to physical processes.
