**Date:** 2026-08-21

## The Universality Class C2 Belongs To

The C2 transport layer is a member of:

    Symmetric Massless Markovian Persistent Random Walk
    = Goldstein-Kac Telegrapher = Maxwell-Cattaneo Equation

All members share: ballistic speed c, Poisson scattering at rate 2γ, Lorentzian
PSD, exponential/K₀ spatial correlations, mean free path relation ξ = c/γ.

C2 introduces no new mathematical structure at the transport layer.
It is a member of this class, not a distinguished member.

## What C2 Adds Over a Generic Telegrapher

**1. c ≤ v_s from FPUT admissibility.**
Equivalent to: coarse-grained excitations cannot exceed acoustic phonon speed.
This is a generic lattice causality bound (Lieb-Robinson analogue).
Not unique to C2. Standard phonon physics gives the same bound.

**2. The uniqueness theorem: FPUT + C1–C6 → telegrapher is forced.**
This is a genuine theorem (Result G-1). It says the telegrapher is the only
admissible projection of FPUT under the stated constraints.
But it delivers you to the universality class — it does not produce a transport
observable that exits the class.

## Conclusion

**C2 is one microscopic realization of the telegrapher universality class.**

At the transport layer it is indistinguishable from standard phonon heat transport,
generic symmetric PRW, or any other telegrapher-class model.

C2 does not currently distinguish itself at the level of transport observables.

Any genuine novelty would have to lie in the foundational/categorical layer
(CCA, SCC) — a separate question outside the scope of this transport audit.
