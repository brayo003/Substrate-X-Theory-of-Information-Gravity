I would formulate the current hypothesis as:

X is an always-present informational field whose local state represents the physically available configuration of the system. Physical causes constrain the configurations available to X; X consequently evolves through the remaining allowed configurations. That is the candidate.

Not:

mass pulls substrate.

physical configuration restricts X's local configuration space, and the resulting constraint produces directional X evolution.

That distinction is enormous.
