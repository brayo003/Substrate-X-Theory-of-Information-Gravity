## FLRW on the W₃ Term

FLRW: ds² = −dt² + a(t)² (dx² + dy² + dz²). Conformally flat. Weyl tensor: C_{αβγδ} = 0 exactly.

W₃|_{FLRW} = 0 exactly (since W₃ is cubic in C, and C = 0 for FLRW).

δW₃/δg^{μν}|_{FLRW}: The variation P_{μν} involves products of C and its derivatives. On FLRW where C = 0:

  P_{μν}^{alg}|_{FLRW} ~ C·C·C terms = 0
  P_{μν}^{der}|_{FLRW} ~ ∇²(C·C) and ∇⁴C terms evaluated at C = 0 = 0

Therefore: δW₃/δg^{μν}|_{FLRW} = 0.

**FLRW satisfies the W₃ equation of motion trivially.** The W₃ term contributes nothing to the FLRW equation of motion — FLRW is still a saddle of the effective action including W₃.

**FLRW admission: CONFIRMED.**

## Near-FLRW Perturbations

For small perturbations δg_{μν} around FLRW: C_{αβγδ} = O(δg). Then W₃ = O(δg)³ and P_{μν} = O(δg)². The W₃ contribution to the perturbed equations of motion is suppressed by (δg)² relative to the Einstein-Hilbert contribution. For δT/T ~ 10^{-5}, the W₃ correction is ~ 10^{-10} — completely negligible.

**Near-FLRW physics is unaffected by W₃.**
