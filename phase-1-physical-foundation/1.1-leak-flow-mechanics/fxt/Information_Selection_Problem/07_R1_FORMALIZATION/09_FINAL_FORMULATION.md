## What the Audit Established

**From 01 (Initial data space):** Γ_GR = C/Diff(Σ) has a symplectic structure but no canonical probability measure.

**From 02 (Measure audit):** No candidate measure on Γ_GR satisfies all requirements (defined, gauge-invariant, finite, assigns different weight to FLRW vs Kasner, independent of a prior state). The phrase "small region of phase space" in R1-original is not mathematically defined.

**From 03 (Weyl/entropy audit):** The Penrose WCH (S_grav ~ C²) is contested. R1 can be stated without it.

**From 04 (Selection vs dynamics):** R1 requires Category 1 (initial-state selection) or Category 3 (quantum-state selection from first principles). Dynamical attraction (Category 2) shifts but does not close R1.

**From 05 (Kasner role):** Excluding Kasner is necessary but not sufficient. R1's target is the full class of high-Weyl configurations, quantified against an independently motivated threshold.

**From 06 (No-go):** A partial structural no-go exists for local classical conditions: any condition independent of C and satisfying Ricci-flat vacuum data is satisfied by Kasner. Full no-go not provable. Gap: nonlocal, quantum, non-geometric conditions.

**From 07 (Reformulations):** R1-D is the strongest mathematically precise formulation without requiring WCH or a canonical measure.

**From 08 (SXC-IGC):** None of the six required capabilities is currently derived in SXC-IGC.

## The Final Formulation

**R1 (final):**

"The correct theory of quantum gravity possesses a physical initial-data sector Γ_phys ⊊ Γ_GR such that all elements of Γ_phys have Weyl curvature C² < C²_threshold at the Planck epoch, where C²_threshold is a value determined by the theory itself (not imported from the desired outcome). No currently known theory derives this sector.

Equivalently: no mechanism derives, from premises that do not encode low Weyl curvature, homogeneity, or isotropy, why the pre-inflationary universe was in a configuration with near-zero Weyl curvature rather than a Kasner, Bianchi IX, or generic inhomogeneous high-Weyl configuration."

---

## What This Formulation Requires

A solution to R1 must provide:

1. A physical theory T (not necessarily GR — it could be a theory from which GR emerges)
2. A definition of Γ_phys within T: the set of configurations realized by T at the Planck epoch
3. A proof that Γ_phys ⊊ Γ_GR
4. A proof that all elements of Γ_phys have C² < C²_threshold
5. C²_threshold derived from T (e.g., C²_threshold ~ M_Planck⁴ if T is Planck-scale physics)
6. A proof that the definition of Γ_phys does not encode C² < C²_threshold in its premises

Items 3 and 4 constitute the formal requirement. Item 6 is the firewall condition.

---

## What This Formulation Does NOT Require

- A probability measure on Γ_GR (requirement removed — Γ_phys ⊊ Γ_GR does not need a measure)
- The Penrose WCH (gravitational entropy is not mentioned)
- A quantum state as input (the theory T must derive it, not import it)
- A preferred background metric
- Homogeneity or isotropy as inputs

---

## Status of R1 Under This Formulation

R1 is **mathematically well-posed** under the final formulation. The required object (Γ_phys ⊊ Γ_GR with C² < C²_threshold) is precisely defined once a theory T is specified. The condition is falsifiable: any proposed T either derives this sector or it does not.

R1 is **open**: no existing theory satisfies all six requirements simultaneously. The closest is ekpyrosis (wide-basin dynamical attractor for Weyl suppression, established in GR) but it fails requirement 4 in the pre-contraction regime and requirement 6 for the pre-contraction initial condition.
