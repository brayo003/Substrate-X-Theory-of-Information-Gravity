# INITIAL_CONDITION_PROPOSALS
## Gate 2 — Dedicated Initial-Condition Proposals

---

### Purpose

Catalog all known dedicated proposals for explaining the cosmological initial condition — frameworks whose explicit purpose is to explain *why* the universe started in a special state.

This is distinct from frameworks (GR, ΛCDM, inflation) that take initial conditions as input. These proposals claim to derive or select the initial conditions.

---

## Section 1: Hartle-Hawking No-Boundary Proposal

**Core claim:** The wave function of the universe is uniquely determined by summing over all compact Euclidean 4-geometries. No boundary = no initial condition to specify. The initial state is selected by quantum gravity.

**What it claims to explain:** The smooth, near-FLRW initial state emerges as the most probable state under the no-boundary wave function.

**Current status of the claim:**
- Penrose argues that the no-boundary proposal predicts *high* entropy initial states (smooth saddle points are exponentially suppressed)
- Hawking-Hertog (2018) revised the analysis, arguing inflation is predicted
- The debate is unresolved; the result depends on which saddle points dominate the path integral and on the contour prescription
- The path integral is not rigorously defined; the Euclidean approach requires analytic continuation whose validity is not established in quantum gravity

**Verdict:** SERIOUS CANDIDATE but contested and formally incomplete.

---

## Section 2: Vilenkin Tunneling Proposal

**Core claim:** The universe tunneled from a state with no classical spacetime ("nothing") to a de Sitter-like space. The tunneling probability selects initial conditions.

**Difference from Hartle-Hawking:** Uses outgoing boundary conditions at the singularity (instead of Hartle-Hawking's regularity condition). Gives different (opposite sign in exponent) predictions in some regimes.

**Problems:**
- "Nothing" with a Hamiltonian is ill-defined
- The boundary condition is a choice, not a derivation
- Does not uniquely predict the observed initial conditions

**Verdict:** SPECULATIVE — boundary condition assumed, not derived.

---

## Section 3: Conformal Cyclic Cosmology (Penrose CCC)

**Core claim:** The universe undergoes endless aeons. The conformally rescaled end of each aeon is the smooth beginning of the next. The initial low-entropy state is explained by the conformal geometry inherited from the previous aeon.

**What it predicts observationally:** Concentric circles of anomalous temperature in the CMB (from black hole collisions in the previous aeon).

**Observational status:** Some groups (Gurzadyan-Penrose) claim detection; others (Moss, Scott, Zibin) find no statistically significant signal above noise. The claimed signal is disputed.

**Verdict:** SPECULATIVE COSMOLOGICAL MODEL — makes predictions but they are disputed.

---

## Section 4: Shape Dynamics (Barbour-Koslowski-Mercati)

**Core claim:** In shape-dynamics, the universe evolves from a state of minimum complexity (Janus point) in both time directions. The arrow of time emerges from the dynamical structure without a special initial boundary condition.

**What it claims:** The "initial" low-complexity state is not an imposed boundary condition but a dynamical attractor (the minimum of the complexity functional on shape space).

**Limitations:**
- Shape dynamics is not fully compatible with GR (it works for specific matter content and in specific regimes)
- The complexity functional must be defined, and its form is a choice
- Quantitative predictions for CMB or large-scale structure have not been derived

**Verdict:** ALTERNATIVE FRAMEWORK — interesting but underdeveloped for quantitative cosmological predictions.

---

## Section 5: Eternal Inflation / Multiverse

**Core claim:** The universe is part of an eternally inflating multiverse. All possible initial conditions are realized in different bubble universes. Our initial conditions are explained anthropically (or by some selection principle among vacua).

**Problems:**
- Not falsifiable in principle (other bubbles are causally disconnected)
- Anthropic arguments do not make unique predictions
- The measure on the multiverse (needed to make probabilistic predictions) is not defined and may not be definable

**Verdict:** NOT A SCIENTIFIC EXPLANATION in the conventional sense — unfalsifiable and measure-undefined.

---

## Section 6: Past-Future Symmetric Cosmologies

**Core claim:** The universe has symmetric initial and final conditions. The apparent low-entropy beginning is explained by imposing time-symmetric boundary conditions (Gold universe, two-state-vector formalism extended to cosmology).

**Problems:**
- Requires a specific final condition as well, which is also unexplained
- Makes unusual predictions about the distant future (entropy decreasing as the future boundary is approached)
- Not consistent with current observations unless the future boundary is cosmologically far away

**Verdict:** ALTERNATIVE — not empirically supported.

---

## Section 7: The July 2026 Information-Saturation Paper

**Core claim:** An information-saturation interface within GR selects FLRW geometries, suppressing Weyl curvature. The early cosmological state is selected by an information-theoretic structural constraint.

**What the authors claim:**
- Their constraint is derived within GR
- It selects FLRW-like geometries
- It provides a structural explanation for Weyl suppression

**What the authors acknowledge:**
- The work is a structural selection principle, not a fully specified dynamical theory
- Quantitative cosmological predictions are deferred to future work
- The framework requires further development

**Questions for adversarial examination (see 03_INFORMATION_LITERATURE/INFORMATION_SATURATION.md):**
- Does the constraint select FLRW independently, or does it encode FLRW in its definition?
- Can an adversarial mathematician construct a wildly non-FLRW spacetime satisfying the constraint?
- What exactly is the "information" being saturated? Is it derived or assumed?
- Does the constraint produce any prediction beyond information → FLRW?

**Verdict:** RECENT SERIOUS CANDIDATE — but explicitly incomplete and requiring adversarial examination before being counted as an explanation.

---

## Section 8: Gate 2 Verdict Summary

| Proposal | Status | Addresses Initial-State Problem? |
|---|---|---|
| Hartle-Hawking no-boundary | Contested, formally incomplete | Claims to — result disputed |
| Vilenkin tunneling | Speculative | Claims to — boundary condition assumed |
| Penrose CCC | Speculative | Claims to — observational signal disputed |
| Shape dynamics | Alternative framework | Partially — underdeveloped |
| Eternal inflation/multiverse | Not falsifiable | Evades rather than explains |
| Past-future symmetric | Not supported | Shifts problem to final condition |
| July 2026 information-saturation | Recent, explicitly incomplete | Claims to — must be adversarially examined |

**Conclusion:** No existing initial-condition proposal is established as a derivation of the low-entropy cosmological initial state. Each either is speculative, formally incomplete, or contested. The no-boundary proposal and the July 2026 paper are the most serious candidates and must be the primary targets of the Gate 3 residual audit.
