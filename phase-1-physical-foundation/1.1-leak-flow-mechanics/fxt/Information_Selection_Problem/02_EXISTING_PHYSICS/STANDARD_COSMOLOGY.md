# STANDARD_COSMOLOGY
## Gate 2 — What ΛCDM Explains About the Initial State

---

### Purpose

Assess what the standard cosmological model (ΛCDM) explains and what it explicitly imports as unexplained assumptions.

---

## Section 1: What ΛCDM Is

ΛCDM is a framework built on:
- GR with a cosmological constant Λ (dark energy)
- Cold dark matter (CDM) as the dominant matter component
- FLRW metric as the background (assumed, not derived)
- An inflationary epoch (incorporated in extended ΛCDM)
- Initial conditions: a nearly scale-invariant primordial power spectrum P(k) ~ k^(n_s - 1)
- Standard Model matter content

ΛCDM is an extraordinarily successful **fitting framework**. It describes the CMB power spectrum, large-scale structure, BBN abundances, expansion history, and many other observations with ~6 free parameters.

**But ΛCDM is a fitting model, not an explanatory theory of initial conditions.** Its success at fitting observations does not mean it explains why those initial conditions existed.

---

## Section 2: What ΛCDM Assumes (Must Be Listed Explicitly)

| Assumption | Status |
|---|---|
| FLRW metric as background geometry | **Assumed** — not derived from more primitive principles |
| Spatial flatness (Ω_k = 0) | **Assumed** (or explained by inflation, which is itself an assumption) |
| Near-scale-invariant primordial power spectrum | **Assumed** (parametrized, not derived from first principles) |
| Initial conditions at the end of inflation | **Assumed** (inflation outputs depend on inflation model) |
| Cosmological constant Λ | **Assumed** — its value has no explanation in ΛCDM |
| Dark matter existence and properties | **Assumed** — no confirmed particle identification |
| Primordial helium fraction from BBN | **Predicted** (given standard model + ΛCDM thermal history) |

---

## Section 3: Assessment Matrix

| Target Feature | ΛCDM explains? | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Large-scale geometric smoothness | **Reframes** | ΛCDM postulates the FLRW background; smoothness is input, not output |
| Small primordial anisotropies (~10⁻⁵) | **Parametrizes** | The amplitude A_s is a free parameter fit to data; not derived |
| Thermalized matter/radiation | **Explains** (given FLRW + SM) | The thermal history of the universe is correctly predicted given the assumed initial conditions |
| Suppressed gravitational DOF | **Does not address** | ΛCDM works in the FLRW background where gravitational DOF are suppressed by assumption |
| Subsequent growth of structure | **Explains** | Structure formation from the assumed P(k) is correctly described |
| Macroscopic entropy increase | **Does not explain** | The thermodynamic arrow is not addressed; ΛCDM assumes the Past Hypothesis implicitly |

---

## Section 4: The Hubble Tension as a ΛCDM Stress Point

The Hubble tension (H₀ ≈ 67.4 from CMB vs ~73 from local measurements) is a ~5σ discrepancy unresolved within standard ΛCDM. Its implications:

- May indicate new physics beyond ΛCDM at z ~ 0.1–2
- May indicate systematic errors in distance measurements
- Does not directly bear on the initial-state problem (which concerns z >> 1100)
- But it does indicate ΛCDM is not a complete description of all epochs

For Gate 2 purposes: ΛCDM is assessed as-is, noting the tension as a caveat.

---

## Section 5: What ΛCDM Does Contribute

ΛCDM provides:
1. A precise quantitative account of cosmic evolution from z ≈ 1100 to today
2. Successful predictions of CMB peak positions and amplitudes
3. Successful predictions of BBN light element abundances (mostly)
4. Correct description of structure growth via linear and nonlinear perturbation theory
5. A framework for testing deviations from standard initial conditions

---

## Gate 2 Verdict for ΛCDM

**ΛCDM does not explain the cosmological initial-state problem. It parametrizes it.**

The initial conditions are free parameters in ΛCDM. Fitting those parameters to data is not an explanation of why those initial conditions existed. The residual problem (why these specific initial conditions?) survives after ΛCDM is granted in full.
