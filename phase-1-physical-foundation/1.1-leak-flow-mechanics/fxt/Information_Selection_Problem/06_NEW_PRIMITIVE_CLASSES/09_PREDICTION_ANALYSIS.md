## Prediction Analysis for New Mechanism Classes

**Date:** August 2026

---

## NC-1 — Constraint Propagation (Cotton-York)

The condition Y^{ij} = 0 restricts the initial extrinsic curvature. In cosmological perturbation theory, Y^{ij} ≠ 0 corresponds to specific non-scalar modes of K_ij (vector and tensor perturbations with spatial gradients).

**Predicted observable:** Suppression of vector-type extrinsic curvature perturbations at the initial slice. In standard cosmology, vector perturbations decay during expansion — they are already suppressed by the time of CMB formation. NC-1 would redundantly suppress them at the initial time, producing no distinguishable difference from the standard scenario.

**Verdict: No distinguishable prediction.**

---

## NC-2 — Quantum Extension Completeness

The condition admits all LQC-bounceable configurations, including Kasner. It does not produce a different set of initial conditions from what LQC already assumes. The predictions of NC-2 are identical to the predictions of LQC applied to all Bianchi models — which are already in the literature.

**Verdict: No new prediction beyond existing LQC predictions.**

---

## General Conclusion

Neither surviving class produces a prediction distinguishable from ΛCDM + inflation + ekpyrosis + standard quantum cosmology.

Both classes either (a) impose conditions already satisfied by standard cosmological initial data, or (b) produce restrictions already implicit in existing quantum cosmology frameworks.
