### Purpose

This file records the formal termination of the program at any gate.

A kill is not a failure. It is a scientific result. The program was designed to be killable, and being killed early (at Gate 3 or 4) is more valuable than surviving to an unfalsifiable speculation.

## Section 1: Kill Conditions by Gate

### Kill at Gate 3 — NO RESIDUAL
The cosmological initial-state problem is already explained by existing physics, or the apparent gap is a misidentification of the problem.

**Scientific meaning:** The literature (including the July 2026 paper or the established no-boundary proposal) has already resolved the problem. This program was not needed. The correct scientific conclusion is: the problem is solved; no new ontology required.

**This is the best possible kill.** It means existing physics is more powerful than it appeared.

### Kill at Gate 4 — Standard Extension Closes the Residual
An existing physical framework, with a standard extension, closes the residual without requiring new ontology.

**Scientific meaning:** A known physical principle was overlooked or underappreciated. Identifying it is a positive contribution.

### Kill at Gate 5 — No Primitive Survives Kill Tests
All candidate primitives either reduce to assumption laundering, are equivalent to existing proposals, or cannot be derived from permitted inputs.

**Scientific meaning:** The residual exists but cannot be addressed by an information-theoretic primitive. The problem may require a different approach (quantum gravity, modified initial conditions, etc.).

### Kill at Gate 6 — Derivation Fails
A primitive survives Gate 5 but cannot generate cosmological dynamics consistent with observation, or cannot generate any dynamics at all (Type 1 only — structural selection without predictions).

**Scientific meaning:** The primitive selects the initial state but does not constitute a complete physical theory. It may still be a useful structural result (like the July 2026 paper), but it is not what this program sought.

### Kill at Gate 7 — No Unique Predictions
The primitive generates predictions, but they are all degenerate with ΛCDM or existing alternative theories.

**Scientific meaning:** The framework may be correct but is empirically equivalent to something already known. It has no testable advantage.

### Kill at Measurement — Prediction Falsified
The framework survived Gate 7 but its predictions disagree with experimental data.

**Scientific meaning:** The framework is wrong. The scientific record should note which predictions were falsified and what they imply about which assumptions to revise.

## Section 3: What Happens After a Kill

After a program kill, the `fxt/` adversarial knowledge base is updated with:
1. The kill record (this file)
2. The specific failure mode that caused the kill
3. The specific assumption or derivation step that was found invalid

This record joins the `fxt/` catalog of demonstrated failure modes. It is as valuable as a survival, because the next attempt at the problem can start from a better-informed position.
