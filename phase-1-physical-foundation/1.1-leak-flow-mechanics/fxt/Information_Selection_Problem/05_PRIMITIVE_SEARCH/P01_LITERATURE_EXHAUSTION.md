## AS + Cosmological Initial Conditions

**Bonanno-Reuter (2002), Phys.Rev.D 65, 043508**
"Cosmology of the Planck era from a renormalization group for quantum gravity."
Applies running G(k), Λ(k) to Friedmann equations with k ~ 1/t. Modifies early-universe evolution, removes big bang singularity in FLRW. Does NOT address initial conditions — FLRW is assumed throughout.
**Relevant to P01: No.**

**Platania (2019), Eur.Phys.J.C 79, 470**
"The inflationary mechanism in Asymptotic Safety."
Studies inflation within AS framework. The inflaton is present; AS modifies the effective potential. Does not address pre-inflationary initial conditions or Weyl suppression.
**Relevant to P01: No.**

**Platania-Wetterich (2019), Phys.Lett.B 811, 135852**
Tensor spectrum modification from AS running G. Discussed in PREDICTION_RESULTS. Does not address initial-state selection.
**Relevant to P01: No.**

**Wetterich (2019), arXiv:1901.04741**
"Cosmology from pregeometry." Attempts to derive cosmology from a fundamental quantum-gravity setting. Does not produce Weyl suppression or initial-state selection.
**Relevant to P01: No.**

**Knorr-Saueressig (2022), arXiv:2202.12172**
"Towards reconstructing the quantum effective action of gravity." Extends the truncation basis. Does not address cosmological initial states.
**Relevant to P01: No.**

## AS + Weyl Curvature

**Falls-Litim (2014), Phys.Rev.D 89, 084002**
Computes β* > 0 for the C² coupling. The paper is about BH physics (AS-improved Schwarzschild). The Weyl-squared coupling is a by-product of the truncation. The paper does not address cosmological initial states or Kasner.
**Relevant to P01: Yes (source of β* value). Does not claim initial-state selection.**

**Ohta-Percacci (2016), Class.Quant.Grav. 33, 035001**
Confirms β* > 0. Studies quadratic gravity fixed points. Does not address cosmological initial conditions.
**Relevant to P01: Yes (source of β* value). Does not claim initial-state selection.**

**Knorr-Platania (2022), Phys.Rev.D 106, L021901**
"Sifting quantum black holes through the principle of least action." Studies how AS modifies BH solutions. Focuses on the BH interior. Does not address cosmological initial data.
**Relevant to P01: Peripheral.**

## AS + Kasner

## Summary

The AS literature that is relevant to P01's proposed R1-closing mechanism consists entirely of:
1. Two papers establishing β* > 0 in truncations (Falls-Litim 2014; Ohta-Percacci 2016).
2. AS-improved FLRW cosmology papers (Bonanno-Reuter 2002; Platania 2019) that assume FLRW throughout.

There is no published work:
- Connecting AS to Kasner or Bianchi models
- Claiming AS suppresses anisotropy or Weyl curvature in initial conditions
- Deriving an AS quantum cosmological state
- Computing the C³ or higher Weyl-polynomial fixed-point couplings relevant to Gap 2

The literature gap is complete: P01's proposed mechanism for R1 is not present in any existing publication. The mechanism is an extrapolation from β* > 0 to "initial-state selection" that has not been made in the literature.
