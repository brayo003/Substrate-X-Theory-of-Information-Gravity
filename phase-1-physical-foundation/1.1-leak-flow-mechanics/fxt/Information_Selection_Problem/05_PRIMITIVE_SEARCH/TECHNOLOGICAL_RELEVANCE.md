## P01 — Asymptotic Safety

**Current level: 2 (conditional)**

Justification:
- The AS program produces cosmological consequences (modified power spectrum, low-ℓ cutoff, trans-Planckian absence) — this is Level 2.
- The Higgs mass prediction (Shaposhnikov-Wetterich 2010) was a Level 3 prediction that was confirmed — but this is a consequence of AS applied to the Standard Model running, not directly of the R1-closing mechanism.
- The massive spin-2 ghost (if M_ghost < M_Planck) is in principle a Level 3 prediction (gravitational wave resonance). But the consistent parameter range pushes M_ghost to Planck scale, making it inaccessible.
- No Level 4 (laboratory) consequence is established.
- No Level 5 consequence follows.

**Level: 2 — New cosmological prediction (conditional on calculation of low-ℓ cutoff).**

The claim cannot exceed Level 2 without:
- A specific, quantitative calculation of the AS-modified primordial power spectrum distinguishable from inflation
- Confirmation that the observed CMB low-ℓ anomaly is physical and matches the AS prediction

## P06 — Anomaly-Freedom

**Current level: 1**

Justification:
- P06 is a mathematical consistency requirement. It is independently motivated and establishes the structure of the physical Hilbert space.
- In the full theory context (which does not exist), it might produce a restriction on the initial-data space — but this has not been shown.
- In the minisuperspace approximation (LQC), it produces a quantum bounce — a Level 2 consequence studied under LQC, not under P06 as stated.
- As an R1-closing mechanism, P06 has no derived consequences at current theoretical development.

**Level: 1 — New mathematical framework (the anomaly-free physical Hilbert space of quantum GR) with no established empirical consequence specific to R1.**
