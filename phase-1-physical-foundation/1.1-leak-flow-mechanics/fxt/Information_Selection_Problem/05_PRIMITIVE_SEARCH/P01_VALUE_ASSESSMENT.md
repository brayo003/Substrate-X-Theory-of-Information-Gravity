## What Would Be Learned from Each Result

### If β* converges to O(10^{-2}) in all truncations

**Established:** The AS fixed point has a small but positive Weyl-squared coupling. This is a quantitative result about the UV structure of quantum gravity.

**Implication for R1:** P01 is eliminated. The O(10^{-2}) suppression of Kasner relative to FLRW (factor ~1.01) is insufficient for any physical selection.

**Scientific value:** The convergence of β* at O(10^{-2}) would establish that the AS fixed point does not have a large Weyl-squared coupling — ruling out a class of quantum gravity models with strong Weyl suppression. This is useful negative information for the AS program itself.

**Classification: Quantum-gravity methodology / foundational physics.**

### If β* >> 1 (β* ~ O(10)–O(10²)) in the full theory

**Established:** The AS fixed point produces exponential suppression of high-Weyl configurations. This would be a quantitative result with cosmological consequences.

**Implication for R1:** Gap 1 partially closed. P01 upgrades to stronger conditional candidate.

**Scientific value:** A large β* would predict: (a) specific running of gravitational waves in the Planck epoch; (b) a Planck-scale UV cutoff on the Weyl sector specifically (not all curvature); (c) modifications to BH physics near the singularity. These are not currently testable but would be of high theoretical value for quantum gravity phenomenology.

**Classification: Cosmological phenomenology / quantum-gravity methodology.**

### If Kasner is removed as a saddle (C³ mechanism, Gap 2)

**Established:** The full AS effective action has a specific structure that excludes Kasner-type solutions. This would be a genuinely new result in mathematical physics — it would mean that the AS fixed point selects a specific class of geometries beyond mere GR solutions.

**Implication for R1:** If Kasner is removed as a saddle AND the excluded set contains all high-Weyl vacuum solutions, P01 upgrades substantially. The distinction between FLRW and Kasner would be a derived result, not an assumed input.

**Scientific value:** This would establish a new selection principle in quantum gravity — the AS effective action as a geometric filter. It would have applications to BH singularity resolution, cosmological initial conditions, and the mathematical structure of higher-derivative gravity theories.

**Classification: Mathematical physics / foundational physics / cosmological phenomenology.**

### If Kasner survives every truncation up to and including C⁶

**Established:** The pattern B_{μν} = 0 for vacuum solutions is robust against higher-derivative corrections up to order 6. This would confirm that the saddle-point inheritance problem (all vacuum GR solutions remain saddles) is a structural feature of the AS effective action, not an artifact of low-order truncations.

**Implication for R1:** P01 is eliminated. The structural obstruction is confirmed: any path-integral-based mechanism inherits all classical GR solutions as saddle points, and the R1 problem cannot be addressed by a weighting mechanism within AS.

**Scientific value:** This negative result would be scientifically valuable. It would establish a clear no-go theorem: **asymptotic safety, through its path-integral weighting mechanism, cannot solve the cosmological initial-state problem.** This would redirect the field toward non-path-integral mechanisms for initial-state selection.

**Classification: Foundational physics / quantum-gravity methodology.**
