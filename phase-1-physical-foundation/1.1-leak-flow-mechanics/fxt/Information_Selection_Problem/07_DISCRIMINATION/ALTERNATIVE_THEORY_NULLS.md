## Section 1: Why Alternative Theory Nulls Are Required

ΛCDM is not the only model consistent with current data. Several alternative frameworks also make predictions about the early universe. A prediction from this program's primitive that distinguishes it from ΛCDM might still be degenerate with a prediction from an alternative theory.

A genuine discriminator must be distinguishable from ALL plausible alternative models, not just from ΛCDM.

## Section 2: Alternative Theory Null Set

### Null Model 1 — Inflationary models beyond slow-roll

Single-field inflation with features (steps, bumps in the potential), multi-field inflation, non-Gaussianity generating models, axion monodromy inflation.

**What they can produce:** Non-standard power spectra, non-zero f_NL, running spectral index, specific r values.

**Distinguishability requirement:** Any prediction from the primitive must not be achievable by tuning inflation model parameters.

### Null Model 2 — Modified Gravity (f(R), scalar-tensor, etc.)

Modified gravity theories alter the Friedmann equations and the primordial power spectrum.

**What they can produce:** Modified expansion history, altered CMB power spectrum at large scales, modified gravitational lensing.

**Distinguishability requirement:** If the primitive modifies the gravitational dynamics, it must produce a signature that differs from all f(R) and scalar-tensor theories.

### Null Model 3 — Bouncing/Cyclic Cosmologies

Pre-big-bang scenarios, ekpyrotic/cyclic models, loop quantum cosmology bounce.

**What they can produce:** Blue-tilted tensor spectrum, specific non-Gaussianity signatures, absence of primordial gravitational waves.

**Distinguishability requirement:** If the primitive selects smooth initial states via a bounce mechanism, it must differ from existing bounce models.

### Null Model 4 — Holographic Cosmology (McFadden-Skenderis)

Alternative to inflation using a 3D holographic dual.

**What it can produce:** Power spectrum consistent with CMB for specific dual QFT parameters.

**Distinguishability requirement:** If the primitive has a holographic character, its predictions must differ from the McFadden-Skenderis dual QFT predictions.

### Null Model 5 — The July 2026 Information-Saturation Paper

The most direct competitor. When its predictions are available, they define the primary alternative null.

**Distinguishability requirement:** The primitive's predictions must differ from those of the July 2026 framework in at least one measurable observable.

## Section 4: The Hierarchy of Evidence

| Evidence level | What it means |
|---|---|
| Prediction differs from all null models | Strong discriminator — proceed to experimental design |
| Prediction differs from ΛCDM but not from some alternatives | Weak discriminator — must refine prediction |
| Prediction is consistent with all null models | Not a discriminator — must find a different observable |
| Prediction is inconsistent with established data | Theory is falsified — Gate 7 fails |
