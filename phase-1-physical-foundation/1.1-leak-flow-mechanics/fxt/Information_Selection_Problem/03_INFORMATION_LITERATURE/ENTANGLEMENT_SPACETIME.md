# ENTANGLEMENT_SPACETIME
## Gate 2 — Entanglement, Gravity, and Quantum Information

---

### Purpose

Assess the quantum-information/gravity literature relevant to the second research branch: what information-processing capability does gravity possess? Also assesses whether entanglement-spacetime connections bear on the initial-state problem.

---

## Section 1: Gravitationally Induced Entanglement

**The program:** Can gravity generate entanglement between initially unentangled quantum systems? If yes, this would imply gravity has quantum (or at least non-classical) features.

**Bose et al. (2017) / Marletto-Vedral (2017):** If two masses in superposition become entangled via gravity alone, this implies gravity cannot be described by a classical channel — it must be mediated by quantum degrees of freedom.

**Experimental status:** Not yet confirmed. Requires controlling quantum superpositions of ~10^{-14} kg masses in isolation — near the edge of current technology. Several experimental designs proposed.

**Relevance to initial-state problem:** This tests whether gravity is quantum — which is relevant to whether quantum cosmology frameworks apply. Does not directly address the initial state.

## Section 2: Non-Entanglement-Breaking Channels

**2025 work (referenced in the instruction):** Derived bounds on quantum-state transfer through gravitationally interacting systems, including tests that do not require measuring gravitationally generated entanglement.

**2026 work (referenced in the instruction):** Maps gravitational nonclassicality onto properties of an induced optical channel, asking whether the gravitational interaction can support a non-entanglement-breaking channel.

**What these establish:** The gravitational interaction has specific information-processing capabilities that can be characterized channel-theoretically. This is the "second branch" of the research program.

**Gate 2 assessment:** These are genuine advances in characterizing gravity as an information-processing medium. They are relevant to the second branch (gravity as information channel) but their connection to the first branch (cosmological initial state) is not yet established.

**CHARTER rule applies:** Do not merge these branches until a mathematical relation emerges. Declaring that they share a common substrate before derivation is the C2 error.

## Section 3: ER=EPR (Maldacena-Susskind 2013)

**Claim:** Einstein-Rosen bridges (wormholes) are equivalent to Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen pairs (entangled particles). Entanglement IS the geometrical connection between spacetime regions.

**What it claims:**
- Maximally entangled pairs correspond to a smooth wormhole
- Unentangled regions have no geometric connection
- The geometry of spacetime is built from entanglement

**Current status:** A conjecture, not a theorem. Well-developed in AdS/CFT where it is better supported. Its applicability to our universe (non-AdS) is speculative.

**Relevance to initial state:** If the smooth early universe corresponds to a specific entanglement pattern, this could connect the initial-state problem to quantum information. But this connection is currently speculative and undeveloped.

---

## Section 4: Quantum Error Correction and Spacetime

**Almheiri-Dong-Harlow (2015):** Bulk spacetime regions can be reconstructed from boundary CFT data in a way analogous to quantum error correction. The bulk-boundary relationship has the structure of a quantum error correcting code.

**Relevance:** Suggests that spacetime geometry has a deep connection to quantum information structure. The smooth early-universe geometry might correspond to a specific error-correcting code structure.

**Status:** Established in AdS/CFT. Extension to cosmological spacetimes is active research, not established.

---

## Section 5: Implications for This Program

The quantum-information/gravity literature establishes:

1. Gravity can (in principle) generate and process quantum information — this is the second branch
2. Spacetime geometry is connected to quantum entanglement (in AdS/CFT settings)
3. These connections are best understood in AdS contexts; extension to cosmology requires work

**What is NOT established:**
- That the same information-theoretic structure that governs gravity-as-channel also explains the initial state
- That entanglement → smooth geometry in cosmological (non-AdS) settings
- Any unique prediction from these connections for the early universe

**Gate 2 verdict:** The quantum information/gravity literature is the most technically developed information-theoretic literature touching on gravity. It is the primary source of mathematical tools if a primitive is eventually derived. But it does not currently contain an explanation of the cosmological initial state.
