## Summary Finding

**No information-theoretic approach in the current literature derives the special low-gravitational-entropy initial state without assuming it.**

The approaches divide into three classes:

**Class 1 — Wrong domain:** Holographic approaches (AdS/CFT) establish deep results in AdS spacetimes. Our universe is not AdS. Extension to cosmological (de Sitter) spacetimes is not established.

**Class 2 — Kinematic tools:** Entanglement reconstruction, tensor networks, and similar frameworks require the quantum state as input. They are representation or reconstruction tools, not selection principles. They cannot select the initial state; they require it.

**Class 3 — Structural claims without derivation:** Zheng (2026) and related approaches propose structural admissibility criteria. They argue that IF certain information-theoretic conditions hold, THEN near-symmetric geometries emerge. They do not derive why the conditions hold or why spacetime emergence preserves the symmetry.

**The common gap across all information-theoretic approaches:** The step from "information-theoretic condition is satisfied" to "the specific low-Weyl cosmological initial state is realized" is never derived. It is either assumed (via spacetime emergence hypothesis), imported (via the quantum state), or circumvented (via AdS/CFT in the wrong geometry).

**R1 survives E_information completely.**
