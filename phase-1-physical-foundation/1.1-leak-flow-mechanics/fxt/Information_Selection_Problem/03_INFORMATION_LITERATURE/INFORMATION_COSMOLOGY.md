# INFORMATION_COSMOLOGY
## Gate 2 — Information-Theoretic Approaches to Cosmology

---

### Purpose

Catalog the existing body of work applying information theory to cosmological questions, assess what each explains, and identify gaps.

---

## Section 1: Wheeler's "It from Bit"

**Claim:** Physical reality is fundamentally informational. Every particle and field derives its existence from binary choices — "it from bit."

**Assessment:** A philosophical program, not a physical theory. Does not make falsifiable predictions. Does not derive the cosmological initial state. Cannot be used as a premise in a derivation.

**Gate 2 verdict:** PHILOSOPHICAL FRAMEWORK — not an explanation.

---

## Section 2: Algorithmic Information Cosmology

**Claim:** The universe is the output of a simple program. Low initial complexity is explained by the fact that the simplest programs are most probable (Solomonoff-Levin measure / algorithmic probability).

**What it claims to explain:** The smooth initial state has low Kolmogorov complexity, hence high algorithmic probability. The universe was "likely" to start smooth.

**Problems:**
- Kolmogorov complexity is not computable — cannot be used in physical dynamics
- The Solomonoff-Levin measure is a prior over programs, not a physical measure over spacetime configurations
- The claim that "simplest programs are most probable" requires a reference universal computer, which is not physically defined
- Does not make quantitative predictions for CMB parameters

**Gate 2 verdict:** HEURISTIC — captures something intuitive but is not a physical derivation.

---

## Section 3: Causal Sets and Information

**Causal set theory** discretizes spacetime into a partially ordered set of events. Information is encoded in the causal structure.

**Relevance to initial state:**
- The initial conditions are specified by the causal set structure at the "beginning"
- Some causal set cosmologies propose that the initial simplicity emerges from combinatorial/counting arguments

**Assessment:** An active research program (Bombelli, Lee, Myrheim, Sorkin). Does not yet provide a derivation of the smooth initial state. Makes predictions about Λ (dark energy) that are in rough order-of-magnitude agreement. Not established.

**Gate 2 verdict:** ALTERNATIVE FRAMEWORK — relevant but no established initial-state derivation.

---

## Section 4: Entropic Gravity (Verlinde)

**Claim:** Gravity is not a fundamental force but an entropic effect — it emerges from the tendency of systems to maximize entropy.

**Verlinde (2011):** Newton's law of gravity follows from the holographic principle and entropy maximization applied to a holographic screen.

**What it claims about the initial state:** Implicitly, if gravity is entropic, then the gravitational "entropy" (the tendency toward more disordered states) drives the universe toward clumping. But the initial smooth state is not explained.

**Problems:**
- The derivation assumes the holographic principle and the Unruh temperature, both of which are non-trivial assumptions
- The extension to full GR (not just Newtonian gravity) is contested
- Even if correct, it does not explain why the initial entropic gradient favored a smooth state

**Gate 2 verdict:** CONTESTED ALTERNATIVE — even if correct, does not address the initial-state problem.

---

## Section 5: Integrated Information Theory (IIT) — Cosmological Extension

**IIT (Tononi):** Consciousness is identical to integrated information Φ. Extended to physics by some authors.

**Cosmological application:** Some have speculated that Φ provides a measure of physical complexity, and that the universe "selects" states with high or low Φ.

**Assessment:** IIT is primarily a theory of consciousness, not cosmology. Its extension to cosmological state selection is highly speculative with no rigorous foundation.

**Gate 2 verdict:** NOT APPLICABLE to the initial-state problem at present.

---

## Section 6: Fisher Information and Quantum Cosmology

**Fisher information** measures the amount of information a random variable carries about a parameter. In quantum mechanics it is related to the quantum Cramér-Rao bound.

**Application to cosmology:** Frieden's "Physics from Fisher Information" program derives physical laws from extremizing Fisher information. Some applications to cosmology have been proposed.

**Assessment:** Frieden's program is controversial (critics argue the derivations are circular or the connection to standard physics is not as claimed). Fisher information is a valid mathematical tool but its elevation to a fundamental principle is not established.

**Gate 2 verdict:** MATHEMATICAL TOOL (legitimate) / PHYSICAL PRINCIPLE (not established).

---

## Gate 2 Summary for Information Cosmology

None of the existing information-theoretic cosmological frameworks constitutes an established, derivation-backed explanation of the low-entropy initial state. They range from philosophical programs (Wheeler) to heuristic arguments (algorithmic) to contested alternative frameworks (entropic gravity).

The surviving gap: an information-theoretic argument that is (a) rigorously defined, (b) derivation-backed, (c) does not assume FLRW/homogeneity, and (d) makes predictions beyond existing physics — does not yet exist in the literature.
