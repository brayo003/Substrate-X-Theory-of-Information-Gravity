## Strongest Mechanism and Why It Still Fails

**Ekpyrotic / slow contraction (Ijjas-Steinhardt 2024, arxiv:2404.00867; Steinhardt-Ijjas 2021, arxiv:2108.07101)**

This is the only mechanism that:
- Operates in full GR (not minisuperspace)
- Is numerically demonstrated (non-perturbative)
- Derives Weyl suppression DYNAMICALLY as an attractor output
- Works from a wide basin of initial conditions (not just perturbatively near FLRW)
- Generates distinguishable predictions (blue-tilted gravitational wave spectrum)

**Why it still does not close R1:**

1. The scalar field φ with potential V(φ) ~ -e^{-cφ} is an assumed input. The existence of this field and the form of its potential are not derived from more fundamental principles.

2. The bounce (transition from slow contraction to expansion) is unresolved in standard GR. It requires new physics — string corrections, loop quantum gravity effects, or other beyond-GR structure — that is not established.

3. The pre-contraction initial state cannot be arbitrarily inhomogeneous. If Weyl curvature is initially too large, black holes form before the ekpyrotic smoothing operates. There is a threshold. Below that threshold, the attractor does its work. Above it, the mechanism fails. The question of why the pre-contraction state was below the threshold is unanswered.

**What ekpyrosis achieves:** It shifts R1 one level deeper. Instead of:

> Why was the big bang smooth?

the residual becomes:

> Why did a slowly contracting scalar field (with the required potential) exist in a state below the black-hole-formation threshold?

This is a genuine shift — not assumption laundering — but it does not eliminate the residual. The residual persists at the deeper level.
