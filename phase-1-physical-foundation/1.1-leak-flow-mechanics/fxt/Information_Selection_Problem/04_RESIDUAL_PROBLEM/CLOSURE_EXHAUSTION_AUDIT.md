# CLOSURE_EXHAUSTION_AUDIT
## R1 Closure Attempt — Full Mechanism Analysis

**R1:** The pre-inflationary gravitational configuration had near-zero Weyl curvature / extremely restricted gravitational phase-space volume. No derivation from independent premises exists.

**Standard:** A mechanism closes R1 only if it DERIVES the special state without importing it as an assumption.

---

## MECHANISM 1: Hartle-Hawking No-Boundary Proposal

**What it is:** Ψ[h_ij] = ∫Dg exp(-S_E[g]) summed over compact Euclidean 4-geometries with no past boundary.

**What it claims:** Smooth, near-de Sitter geometries dominate the path integral and give the universe a low-entropy start.

**The critical problems:**

*The sign problem:* In the original formulation, the dominant saddle gives Ψ ~ exp(+|S_E|), which means *larger* Λ gives *higher* probability — this predicts preferring small universes that undergo minimal inflation. Penrose has consistently argued the no-boundary proposal predicts HIGH-entropy initial states, not low-entropy ones.

*The 2017-2018 Feldbrugge-Lehners-Turok result:* When the Lorentzian path integral is defined with the lapse integrated over the real axis, the perturbation sector gives exponentially growing (unstable) fluctuations. The no-boundary proposal predicts an inhomogeneous universe. Diaz Dorronsoro et al. attempted rescue via complex lapse contour; Feldbrugge-Lehners-Turok (2018, Universe 4:100) showed the complex contour also produces mathematical inconsistencies. The contour debate is unresolved as of 2025-2026 (arxiv:2602.02682 finds probability predictions depend critically on norm choice).

*Minisuperspace truncation:* All calculable results are in minisuperspace (FLRW restricted from the start). Full superspace — which includes inhomogeneous configurations — has never been computed.

**Adversarial answers:**
- Q6 (derives low entropy?): NO — the no-boundary condition IS the boundary condition. It stipulates smooth geometries.
- Q7 (derives low Weyl?): NO — Weyl=0 follows from selecting the de Sitter saddle, which is the saddle of the FLRW sector by construction.
- Q8 (derives homogeneity?): NO — minisuperspace restriction assumes it.
- Q9 (phase-space restriction?): NOT ADDRESSED — full superspace calculation does not exist.
- Q11 (rewritable?): YES — this is a stipulated boundary condition in quantum gravity, not derived from a more primitive principle.

**VERDICT: Does NOT close R1.**
Relocates the question: "why Weyl ≈ 0?" becomes "why the no-boundary condition?" The condition is stipulated. The proposal is additionally in active dispute about whether it even predicts the desired state.

**Failure mode:** ASSUMPTION RELABELED — "no boundary" is "no initial inhomogeneity" restated in path-integral language.

## MECHANISM 2: Vilenkin Tunneling Proposal

**What it is:** Universe tunnels from "nothing" to de Sitter. Wave function uses outgoing boundary conditions at the singularity (opposite sign convention from H-H in some regimes).

**What it claims:** Small, smooth de Sitter universes have highest tunneling amplitude.

**Problems:**
- "Nothing" with a Hamiltonian is self-contradictory — it requires pre-existing quantum mechanical structure.
- All calculations in minisuperspace: FLRW assumed throughout.
- The outgoing boundary condition is stipulated.
- Predictions differ from Hartle-Hawking in sign but share the same structural problem.

**VERDICT: Does NOT close R1.**

**Failure mode:** MINISUPERSPACE TRUNCATION + STIPULATED BOUNDARY CONDITION.

## MECHANISM 3: Carroll-Chen / Eternal Inflation / Measure-Based Explanations

**What it is:** Carroll-Chen (2004, hep-th/0410270): In a universe with a positive cosmological constant and empty de Sitter space as a background, inflation can begin spontaneously in random fluctuations. Eternal inflation then produces a multiverse of expanding regions, each with its own arrow of time. The low-entropy observable universe is one such region.

**What it claims:** The thermodynamic arrow of time is explained without a special initial condition — any typical state in de Sitter will eventually produce inflating regions.

**The critical distinction — the single most important adversarial cut:**

Carroll-Chen explains why LOW-ENTROPY STATES EXIST SOMEWHERE in an eternal multiverse. It does NOT explain why our specific observable universe begins in the required low-entropy gravitational state.

This is the selection problem within the mechanism: given that the multiverse contains all kinds of initial conditions, why does our branch begin with near-zero Weyl curvature rather than a thermal gravitational state? Carroll-Chen has no answer to this. It replaces "our universe started in a special state" with "some branch started in a special state, and we happen to be in that branch."

**The Boltzmann brain problem:** Under the Carroll-Chen mechanism, fluctuations producing a single low-entropy observer (Boltzmann brain) are vastly more probable than fluctuations producing an entire low-entropy observable universe. The mechanism predicts we should be Boltzmann brains. Carroll-Chen acknowledge this and argue that with the right measure, ordinary observers dominate — but the measure choice is not derived.

**The measure problem:** Eternal inflation generates infinitely many regions of every type. Without a well-defined measure on the multiverse, no probability statement can be made about which type of region we should expect to occupy. This problem is explicitly UNSOLVED (see Phys.Rep. on the measure problem; Freivogel 2011; Garriga-Schwartz-Vilenkin et al.). The measure is not derived from first principles.

**Adversarial answers:**
- Q6: NO — the mechanism does not derive low gravitational entropy in our patch; it assumes some patches have it.
- Q9: NO — the enormous phase-space restriction of the initial gravitational state is not explained; it is selected anthropically within an uncontrolled measure.
- Q10: The arrow of time is explained globally (each inflating branch has a local arrow) but not for our specific branch.

**VERDICT: Does NOT close R1.**

**Failure mode:** SELECTION PROBLEM + MEASURE UNDEFINED — explains existence of low-entropy regions but not selection of ours; requires an undefined multiverse measure.

## MECHANISM 4: Barbour-Koslowski-Mercati (BKM) Shape Dynamics / Janus Point

**What it is:** In Newtonian N-body gravity on shape space, all generic solutions pass through a point of minimum complexity (the Janus point). On both sides of this point, complexity increases and each branch has its own arrow of time. The arrow emerges from the dynamics without a special initial condition being imposed.

**What it claims:** The arrow of time and an ordered low-complexity beginning emerge from the structure of Newtonian gravitational dynamics on shape space, without invoking a Past Hypothesis.

**What it actually shows (from arxiv:1310.5167, 1507.06498):**
The result is proven for the Newtonian N-body problem with zero total energy, momentum, and angular momentum. The Janus point is a minimum of the shape complexity functional I_cm/I (ratio of center-of-mass moment of inertia to total moment of inertia). On either side, complexity increases generically.

**Critical limitations for R1:**

*Not GR:* The mechanism is proven for Newtonian gravity on shape space. Extension to full GR cosmology is not established. The shape space of GR initial data is not the same mathematical object as the N-body shape space.

*Complexity ≠ Weyl curvature:* The BKM complexity functional is a measure of N-body shape complexity, not of spacetime Weyl curvature. The mechanism produces a minimum-complexity point in the N-body sense; it does not derive suppressed Weyl curvature of a cosmological spacetime.

*Janus point is a minimum of complexity, not zero Weyl:* The initial state at the Janus point is the configuration of minimum shape complexity — this is roughly a "most spread out" or "most uniform" N-body distribution. This is not the same as Weyl curvature suppression in GR.

*The arrow of time:* BKM correctly identifies that a dynamical arrow emerges from the structure of Newtonian gravity without a special initial condition. This is genuinely interesting. But for R1, the question is Weyl curvature suppression in a GR cosmological context, not the N-body arrow.

*Recent extension:* arxiv:2607.27526 (2026) examines structural morphology and the gravitational arrow. Still restricted to N-body; no connection to Weyl curvature in GR cosmology.

**Adversarial answers:**
- Q6: PARTIALLY — derives low complexity in N-body sense; does not derive low Weyl curvature.
- Q7: NO — Weyl curvature is a GR concept; BKM does not operate in GR cosmology.
- Q8: NO — derives isotropy in N-body sense at Janus point; not the same as FLRW isotropy.
- Q12: YES, genuinely new — shape dynamics is a distinct mathematical framework.
- Q14-15: No quantitative CMB or large-scale structure predictions derived.

**VERDICT: Does NOT close R1 as stated.**

BKM establishes that arrows of time can emerge without a Past Hypothesis in N-body Newtonian gravity. This is a genuine result. It does NOT establish that Weyl curvature is suppressed in GR cosmology without a special initial condition. The extension to GR required for R1 has not been made.

**Failure mode:** DOMAIN MISMATCH — proven result is for Newtonian N-body; R1 requires GR cosmology.

## MECHANISM 5: Ekpyrotic / Slow Contraction (THE STRONGEST EXISTING THREAT TO R1)

**Core claim:** A phase of slow contraction (w >> 1) before the big bang is a stable attractor that drives the universe toward homogeneity, isotropy, and flat geometry, dynamically suppressing Weyl curvature. This is independent of initial conditions over a wide range.

**Key papers:**
- Ijjas & Steinhardt (2024), arxiv:2404.00867: Non-perturbative numerical relativity confirms slow contraction robustly smooths and flattens from initial conditions outside the perturbative FLRW regime. Inflation fails these tests.
- Steinhardt & Ijjas (2021), arxiv:2108.07101: Entropy tracking in cyclic universe — entropy after each bounce is near-maximal in matter-radiation sector and near-minimal in gravitational sector, satisfying Weyl curvature conditions without imposing them.
- arxiv:2607.07815 (2026): Slow contraction admits a stable past attractor asymptoting to Minkowski space AND a stable flat, homogeneous, isotropic future attractor with negligible Weyl curvature.

**What the attractor argument actually shows:**

Given a universe undergoing slow contraction with equation of state w >> 1, the dynamical equations have a stable future attractor that is homogeneous, isotropic, and flat. This attractor is reached from a WIDE BASIN of initial conditions — including highly inhomogeneous and anisotropic starting points (demonstrated numerically in 2404.00867).

This is the most serious existing threat to R1 because:
1. It is a dynamical derivation, not a boundary condition.
2. It operates in full GR, not just minisuperspace.
3. It is demonstrated numerically from non-perturbative initial conditions.
4. It suppresses Weyl curvature as an output, not as an input.
5. The Weyl curvature suppression follows from the w >> 1 attractor, which is independently motivated (scalar field with steep negative potential).

**Adversarial examination of the attractor claim:**

*Q1. Primitive input:* A scalar field φ with a steep negative potential V(φ) < 0 driving slow contraction (w >> 1). This scalar field is postulated.

*Q2. Is the scalar field independently motivated?*
The ekpyrotic scalar is motivated by brane-collision scenarios in string theory, but its existence and potential form are assumed. It is not derived from a more fundamental principle. The potential V ~ -e^{-cφ} with c >> 1 is an input.

*Q6. Does it derive low Weyl curvature?*
DYNAMICALLY DERIVED — given the scalar field and GR, the attractor produces negligible Weyl curvature. This is a genuine derivation within the assumed framework.

*Q7. Does it derive homogeneity without assuming it?*
YES, from a wide basin — the numerical evidence in 2404.00867 shows smoothing from highly inhomogeneous initial conditions.

*Critical remaining question: What is the pre-contraction state?*
The slow contraction phase must begin somewhere. What is the state BEFORE the contraction begins? The attractor is stable in the forward direction (toward the bounce), but the initial state of the contraction phase is not derived — it is assumed to be "generic enough" for the attractor to take hold. Specifically:
- The scalar field must exist and be in the correct part of its potential.
- The universe must already be below a certain threshold of inhomogeneity for the attractor to dominate before the bounce (if the initial Weyl curvature is too large, black holes form before smoothing occurs).

*The black hole problem:* If the pre-contraction universe is highly inhomogeneous (large Weyl curvature), regions collapse into black holes before the ekpyrotic smoothing can operate. The attractor only dominates if the initial state is not too far from smooth. This is the same bootstrap problem inflation has — just with a different threshold.

*The bounce:* The transition from slow contraction to expansion requires a non-singular bounce. No established mechanism for this exists in standard GR. Proposed bounces require new physics (string theory corrections, loop quantum cosmology, etc.) that are not derived.

*The cyclic claim (2108.07101):* Steinhardt-Ijjas argue entropy after each bounce is near-maximal in matter sector and near-minimal in gravitational sector. But this requires:
- The bounce preserves the smooth geometry achieved by contraction.
- Black holes formed during previous cycles are diluted by expansion. The claim is that black hole entropy is sub-dominant to radiation entropy after reheating. This is argued but the tracking through the bounce singularity is not fully established.

*Q11. Rewritable?*
The mechanism uses standard GR + a scalar field. The scalar field is the new ingredient. It is not an information-theoretic primitive — it is a conventional matter field.

*Q14-15. Predictions distinguishable from inflation?*
YES — slow contraction predicts a blue-tilted gravitational wave spectrum (n_T > 0) with very low amplitude, versus inflation's nearly scale-invariant or red-tilted spectrum. This IS potentially distinguishable by future detectors (LISA, BBO, CMB-S4). The perturbation spectrum from ekpyrosis requires a two-field mechanism (entropy perturbations) or the adiabatic mechanism — this generates a nearly scale-invariant scalar spectrum but with non-standard non-Gaussianity.

**VERDICT: PARTIALLY closes R1 — the strongest existing mechanism, but with an unresolved pre-contraction initial state.**

The slow contraction attractor IS a genuine dynamical derivation of Weyl suppression from a wide basin of initial conditions within GR. It is the most serious existing physics mechanism for addressing R1.

**What it fails to close:**
1. The scalar field and its potential are assumed inputs.
2. The initial state of the contraction phase is not derived — it requires "generic enough" conditions for the attractor to operate before black hole formation.
3. The bounce mechanism is unresolved.
4. A fully cyclic version (which would remove any special initial state) requires the bounce to preserve the smooth geometry through each cycle — not established.

**Failure mode:** SHIFTED INITIAL CONDITION — moves R1 from "why was the big bang smooth?" to "why was the pre-contraction scalar field in the right state and why was initial Weyl curvature below the black-hole-formation threshold?"

This is a GENUINE PARTIAL CLOSURE — not a relabeling, but an actual shift of the residual to a deeper level. The residual survives but in a modified form.

## MECHANISM 6: Penrose Conformal Cyclic Cosmology (CCC)

**Core claim:** The universe undergoes infinite aeons. The conformally rescaled future of one aeon is the smooth beginning of the next. The low-entropy start is inherited from the conformal geometry of the previous aeon's end.

**What it assumes:** The conformal rescaling of the end of an aeon — dominated by massless radiation after all matter decays — is smooth. The smoothness is inherited from the late-time de Sitter phase, which is conformally flat.

**The circular structure:** CCC argues the smooth beginning of each aeon arises from the conformally flat end of the previous one. But why was the first aeon's end conformally flat? This either regresses infinitely or requires an independent explanation.

**Observational status (2024):** CMB searches for predicted concentric low-variance circles (from black hole collisions in the previous aeon) find no statistically significant signal after removing bright spots (arxiv:2208.06021). The Hawking point search similarly finds no significant evidence. CCC is observationally unsupported.

**Entropy accounting:** The conformal rescaling maps the high-entropy (high-Weyl, black-hole-dominated) end of one aeon to a low-Weyl beginning of the next. This is possible because the conformal factor diverges at the end (infinite expansion). The Weyl curvature is conformally invariant but the physical entropy is not — the rescaling effectively resets the gravitational degrees of freedom. Steinhardt-Ijjas (2108.07101) note this is essentially an entropy partitioning by conformal geometry, not a physical entropy reduction.

**VERDICT: Does NOT close R1.** CCC relocates the special initial condition to the conformal geometry of the previous aeon's end. Observationally unsupported.

**Failure mode:** INFINITE REGRESSION + NO OBSERVATIONAL SUPPORT.

## MECHANISM 7: Holographic and Entanglement-Based Approaches

**7a. Holographic cosmology (McFadden-Skenderis):** CMB power spectrum reproduced by a 3D CFT dual. Does not address the initial state of the dual CFT — shifts R1 to why the dual QFT is in a special state.

**7b. Island formula / black hole information:** Resolves information paradox for black holes. No established connection to cosmological initial state or Weyl suppression.

**7c. Entanglement → spacetime (Van Raamsdonk, ER=EPR):** Demonstrates entanglement builds spatial connectivity in AdS/CFT. Does not explain why the early cosmological state had specific entanglement structure. Extension from AdS to cosmological (de Sitter) settings is not established.

**7d. Cao-Carroll-Michalakis:** Reconstructs geometry from entanglement in a given quantum state. Requires the quantum state as input — the initial state question is not answered. The reconstruction is kinematic, not a selection principle.

**7e. Entanglement Past Hypothesis:** Proposes low initial entanglement entropy (Carroll, Singh, and collaborators). Requires a subsystem decomposition that presupposes spatial structure — circular for deriving spacetime geometry.

**Common verdict for all holographic/entanglement approaches:** These frameworks establish deep connections between information and geometry in specific (usually AdS) settings. None derives the specific low-Weyl, near-homogeneous initial state of our cosmology without assuming the state's structure. None extends to the pre-inflationary regime in our (de Sitter-like) universe with established rigor.

**VERDICT: No holographic or entanglement-based approach closes R1.**

## MECHANISM 8: Other Information-Theoretic Approaches

**8a. Algorithmic information (Wheeler It-from-Bit, Algorithmic cosmology):** Low initial complexity argued from algorithmic probability. Not computable, not falsifiable, not a physical derivation.

**8b. Fisher information cosmology (Frieden):** Derivation of physical laws from extremizing Fisher information. Widely criticized as circular. Not an initial-state selection principle.

**8c. Entropic gravity (Verlinde):** Gravity as entropic force. Even if correct, does not select initial gravitational state.

**8d. Constructor theory (Deutsch-Marletto):** Reformulates physics in terms of possible/impossible tasks. Has not been applied to the cosmological initial state. Would at most narrow the possibility space, not select the realized state.

**8e. Causal sets:** Discretizes spacetime. Some models argue for initial simplicity from combinatorics. No established quantitative derivation of Weyl suppression.

**8f. Tensor networks / MERA:** Relate entanglement renormalization to holography. Kinematic tools for representing quantum states. Do not select initial states.

**8g. Zheng (2026) — information saturation interface:** Already audited in 03_INFORMATION_LITERATURE/INFORMATION_SATURATION.md. VERDICT: Does NOT close R1. The spacetime emergence step is unproven; the symmetry preservation assumption carries the explanatory weight.

**Common verdict:** No information-theoretic approach in the current literature derives the special gravitational initial state without assuming it. They either reformulate the question, apply only in specific limits (AdS), or postulate rather than derive the required structure.

**VERDICT: No information-theoretic mechanism closes R1.**

## SUMMARY: Strongest Mechanisms and Residual Status

**Strongest existing mechanism:** Ekpyrotic / slow contraction (Mechanism 5)
- Genuine dynamical derivation of Weyl suppression from a wide basin
- Works in full GR, numerically demonstrated
- Distinguishable prediction: blue-tilted gravitational wave spectrum
- **Residual after granting mechanism 5:** Why was the pre-contraction scalar field in the right state? Why was initial Weyl curvature below the black-hole-formation threshold? The bounce mechanism is unresolved.

**Strongest threat to R1:** Ekpyrotic attractor (if the bounce is resolved and the pre-contraction initial state can be shown to be "generic"). If a complete cyclic model is established — with a proven bounce, entropy reset, and wide-basin attractor — R1 would be closed by existing physics.

**Exact unresolved obstruction:**
1. The ekpyrotic scalar field and its potential are assumed inputs (not derived from first principles).
2. The bounce mechanism is unresolved (requires physics beyond standard GR).
3. The pre-contraction initial state must not be too inhomogeneous (black hole formation threshold) — this is a residual initial condition requirement.
4. All quantum cosmology proposals (H-H, Vilenkin) work in minisuperspace and either stipulate smoothness or produce contested predictions.
5. No information-theoretic approach derives the initial state without assuming the symmetry it claims to explain.

**R1 STATUS AFTER FULL AUDIT:**

R1 is PARTIALLY shifted by mechanism 5 (ekpyrosis) but NOT closed. The residual is:

> Why was the pre-contraction state within the attractor basin (i.e., below the black-hole-formation threshold, with the scalar field in the correct region of its potential)?

This is a genuine unresolved structural question that ekpyrosis itself cannot answer without importing a residual initial condition.
