## Section 2: Reading the Matrix

### Features that ARE explained by standard frameworks:
- **Feature 3 (thermalization):** Explained by reheating + BBN + standard model, given that inflation ended. This is a genuine success of the standard picture.
- **Feature 5 (structure growth):** Explained by GR perturbation theory + ΛCDM, given that the initial power spectrum had the measured form.

### Features that are REFRAMED but not explained:
- **Feature 1 (smoothness):** ΛCDM assumes FLRW; inflation explains post-inflationary smoothness but requires pre-inflationary smooth initial conditions; GR permits all geometries.
- **Feature 6 (entropy increase):** Statistical mechanics explains it given low initial entropy — but the low initial entropy is exactly what requires explanation.

### Features with CONTESTED claims but no established explanation:
- **Feature 4 (gravitational DOF suppression):** Penrose's Weyl curvature conjecture, the no-boundary proposal, and the July 2026 information-saturation paper all claim to address this. None is established.

### Features that are PARTIALLY explained (amplitude gap):
- **Feature 2 (primordial anisotropies):** The spectral index n_s ≈ 0.965 follows naturally from slow-roll inflation. The amplitude A_s is a free parameter fit to the inflaton potential — parametrized, not derived from first principles.

## Section 3: What the Matrix Shows

After granting everything in the standard picture (GR + ΛCDM + inflation + reheating + BBN + QFT-CS), the following survive as unexplained or contested:

**GENUINE RESIDUAL CANDIDATES:**
1. Why was the pre-inflationary state smooth enough for inflation to begin? (Features 1, 4)
2. Why was the gravitational entropy low at early times? (Feature 4 — the Weyl/entropy problem)
3. Why was the primordial perturbation amplitude A_s what it was? (Feature 2 — mild residual, may be resolved by specific inflation models)
4. Why does macroscopic entropy increase rather than decrease? (Feature 6 — requires the above to be answered first)

**NOT RESIDUAL:**
- Thermalization of matter/radiation: explained
- Structure growth: explained given initial conditions

## Section 4: The Critical Path

The critical path to Gate 3 is Feature 4: **suppressed gravitational degrees of freedom / low Weyl curvature at early times**.

This is the feature that:
- Inflation explicitly fails to explain (it requires it)
- Statistical mechanics cannot address (no established gravitational entropy)
- The no-boundary proposal claims to explain but is contested
- The July 2026 paper claims to explain but is explicitly incomplete

**If a genuine residual exists, this is where it lives.**
