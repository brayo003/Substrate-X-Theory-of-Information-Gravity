## Key Findings

**Strongest mechanism:** Ekpyrotic / slow contraction — the only mechanism with a genuine dynamical derivation of Weyl suppression from a wide basin in full GR, confirmed numerically (arxiv:2404.00867).

**Why it still fails to close R1:** The mechanism requires (a) an assumed scalar field with a specific potential, (b) an unresolved bounce, and (c) the pre-contraction state to be below a black-hole-formation threshold. The residual initial condition question is shifted, not eliminated.

**No mechanism closes R1 completely.** The residual survives the full audit.
