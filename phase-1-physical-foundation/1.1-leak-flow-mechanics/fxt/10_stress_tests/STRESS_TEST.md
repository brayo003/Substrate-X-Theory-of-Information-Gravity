# Scientific Stress-Test Report

## Scope and reproducibility

This is an adversarial verification report for the frozen repository. It does not modify, extend, or reinterpret the derivations. Every conclusion below is generated by one of the scripts in this directory. Each script writes a JSON summary, an NPZ intermediate-data archive, a log, and a PNG figure under `outputs/`; all random searches have fixed seeds. Run the suite from this directory with `python 01_mathematical_stress.py` through `python 12_publication_readiness.py` in numerical order.

The reference claim tested throughout is the frozen conditional relation

`Gamma_phi = 8 g^2 s0^2 r_q^2 tau omega_rot^2 / (3 hbar^2) = k_rot omega_rot^2`.

“PASS” means the stated numerical/structural criterion passed. “PARTIAL” means an important domain restriction, degeneracy, or missing evidence was established. “FAIL” means the adversarial criterion found counterexamples; it does not by itself invalidate a derivation explicitly limited to its assumptions.

## Executive assessment

The implementation of the effective `sigma_z` GKSL equation is numerically sound. That fact validates the implementation, not the microscopic derivation. The suite finds three major limitations:

- The time-local Markov equation is not supported outside small `tau_B Gamma_phi`; a finite-memory countermodel differs by 0.339 at `tau_B Gamma_phi≈1` and 0.698 at `≈10`.
- Coherence-only Ramsey data do not identify the microscopic mechanism: the Fisher matrix for `(log g, log s0)` is singular, and alternative GKSL channels can closely reproduce the envelope.
- An unrestricted 100,000-point admissible-positive parameter search finds 17.217% failures of at least one stated Markov/finite-memory criterion. Thus there is no evidence for a universal-domain claim.

The result is a publication-grade *conditional verification framework*, but not evidence for a unique new open-system mechanism or for experimental novelty beyond standard Lindblad dynamics.

## 01 — Mathematical stress

The symbolic test verifies exact logarithmic sensitivities: `d log Gamma/d log(g,s0,rq,tau,omega,hbar)=(2,2,2,1,2,-2)`. The zero-coupling, zero-rotation, and zero-correlation-time limits vanish; the expression diverges as `hbar -> 0`, so that singular point is explicitly not a valid numerical regime. The dimensional calculation uses the frozen units for `g`, `s0`, `r_q`, `tau`, and `hbar` and returns a rate dimension.

The dependency graph records that the quadratic time-local rate depends on linear coupling, rotational variance, Born, Markov, stationarity, and the single-exponential correlation reduction. Removing any of the latter four removes the stated derivational route. It would be incorrect to label that a pass for the derivation.

Artifacts: `01_mathematical_stress.*`, `01_approximation_validity.png`.

## 02 — Numerical stress

The test separately searches explicit-Euler failure and tests the actual GKSL implementation with BDF in weak, intermediate, and stiff regimes using 12 random density matrices. The latter preserves trace, Hermiticity, and positivity to the reported bounds. The intentionally unsafe explicit-Euler scan has 29.55% cells above `1e-2` error, documenting why a solver pass is conditional on method and resolution.

Artifacts: `02_numerical_stress.*`, `02_numerical_stability.png`.

## 03 and 06 — Quantum channel and Lindblad benchmark

The target is pure `sigma_z` dephasing with `Gamma_phi=0.7`. It is exactly reproduced by the corresponding standard Lindblad channel—therefore this coincidence cannot be evidence for the microscopic derivation. Amplitude damping produces population motion (excursion `0.493` in the named test), while bit flip, depolarizing, and mixed Pauli models show that population-sensitive measurements are needed in addition to Ramsey coherence.

The broad benchmark evaluates 55 rates by 160 times for each named channel. Its numerical difference maps are useful exclusions, but they are not experimental detectability maps: no frozen source supplies a noise floor, sample size, or apparatus model.

Artifacts: `03_quantum_stress.*`, `03_quantum_channel_map.png`, `06_lindblad_benchmark.*`, `06_*_difference.png`.

## 04 — Inverse problem

The test uses an explicitly labelled synthetic noisy rate-versus-frequency data set only to assess the model geometry. The two Fisher columns are identical. Consequently the information matrix has a zero eigenvalue and infinite condition number: rate data recover the composite coefficient (equivalently `g*s0` when other factors are fixed), not `g` and `s0` separately. The posterior-grid plot is a ridge, not a confidence ellipse with finite axes.

Artifacts: `04_inverse_problem_stress.*`, `04_inverse_problem.png`.

## 05 — Prediction search

The frozen time-local pure-dephasing equation entails the documented Ramsey exponential and the quadratic frequency scaling. It does **not** entail temperature scaling, CPMG pulse-spacing dependence, dynamical-decoupling novelty, multi-qubit scaling, or a noise-spectroscopy signature. In this model the computed Ramsey and echo envelopes coincide; invoking an echo advantage would require a non-Markov/static-noise model not present in the frozen equations.

Artifacts: `05_predictive_stress.*`, `05_predictive_sweep.png`.

## 07 and 08 — Derivation and approximation failure maps

The finite-memory calculation is a deliberately conservative countermodel for the Markov replacement: it retains an exponential bath correlation but does not replace it by a time-local rate. The resulting initial slip quantifies the loss incurred by the reduction. The ablation report distinguishes this computational counterexample from assertions about Born factorization or bath stationarity, which cannot be validated from an already reduced equation.

The green/yellow/red map uses only declared controls: a normalized Born proxy and `tau_B Gamma_phi`. Its thresholds are operational conventions (`<0.01`, `0.01–0.1`, `>=0.1`) displayed in JSON; they must not be mistaken for a new theorem.

Artifacts: `07_derivation_stress.*`, `07_markov_ablation.png`, `08_approximation_stress.*`, `08_approximation_map.png`.

## 09–11 — Structural, falsification, and sensitivity audits

Mixed longitudinal/transverse alternatives show a nonzero population excursion as soon as transverse content is included. This supports conditional selection of an effective longitudinal channel if pure dephasing and energy-basis population conservation are observed. It does not select a unique substrate bath.

The seeded 100,000-point log-uniform adversarial search found a worst `tau Gamma` of `8.18e17` and a 17.217% combined failure rate under pre-registered criteria. This is the suite’s only FAIL and is the appropriate result: a weak-Markov derivation cannot be treated as universal.

Sensitivity makes the hidden-parameter issue explicit: `k_rot` changes with `g`, `s0`, `r_q`, `tau`, and `hbar`; treating it as a constant requires those quantities to be controlled or absorbed into an empirically fitted coefficient.

Artifacts: `09_structural_robustness.*`, `10_falsification_search.*`, `11_sensitivity_analysis.*` and their PNG figures.

## Publication readiness

The machine-generated synthesis is [PUBLICATION_READINESS.md](PUBLICATION_READINESS.md). Its defensible statement is:

> Within declared Born–Markov/stationary assumptions, the frozen rate law has a dimensionally consistent, numerically stable pure-dephasing GKSL implementation. The present evidence does not uniquely identify its microscopic interaction, establish a new prediction beyond standard Lindblad dynamics, or support a universal validity claim.

Further work would require independent observables to break parameter degeneracy, a specified experimental noise model before any detectability claim, and a microscopic non-Markov treatment before extending conclusions into red regions. Those would be new research activities and are intentionally outside this frozen-framework stress suite.
