# v02 Research Charter

## Boundary

v01 remains frozen and is the source of all inherited equations. v02 may add
new hypotheses, but each must be recorded with its physical content, units,
new parameter count, falsifier, and standard competing model.

## Non-negotiable gates

A v02 result cannot be described as a C2 prediction unless all conditions hold:

1. Every new assumption is named and visible.
2. The relation is dimensionally valid in SI units.
3. The bridge constant is either derived from an accepted v02 principle or
   measured independently of the Ramsey/dephasing data used for validation.
4. A standard bath model with the same number of freely fitted parameters has
   been compared on the same data.
5. A prospective falsification condition is stated before data collection.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Defining `eta` from a fitted `k_rot` and calling it derived.
- Identifying an information unit with joules by notation alone.
- Treating a Ramsey fit as an independent calibration of `g`.
- Claiming a QFT completion before an action, field content, symmetry,
  renormalization prescription, and matching observable are specified.

## Promotion rule

Only a result passing the five gates may be proposed for review as a v02
theorem. Until then it is a hypothesis or a measurement plan.
