# Source manifest

## Authoritative source

- `../rota.pdf` — A. A. Wood *et al.*, *Quantum measurement of a rapidly
  rotating spin qubit in diamond*, arXiv:1802.03512v1 (the supplied paper).

This reproduction project uses the supplied PDF as its sole scientific source
until an explicit later decision records an additional source.

## Source handling decisions

- No C2, Substrate-X, H01, or other hypothetical terms are permitted in this
  project.
- The repository contains `../data/Dataset_10.5281zenodo.14697917/`. Its files
  are dated 2022--2023 and describe a different NV dataset. They are **not**
  claimed to be data from Wood *et al.* and are excluded from validation.
- The PDF contains plotted Figure 4 and Figure 5 data but no machine-readable
  table for either. Any later digitization must be stored under `digitized/`
  with its coordinate calibration and uncertainty, and must be labelled
  digitized rather than raw.
