# Gate 11 — additional-contribution readiness review

## Decision

The conventional twin is ready for a later, separately authorised residual
study. No additional term is implemented here.

## What the present data can support

For the 11 digitized Figure 5 points, the fitted conventional residual RMS is
0.046 normalized contrast and the plotted one-standard-error bars range from
about 0.033 to 0.079. This establishes only a **data-space** scale for a
detectable residual in this particular short echo scan.

A minimum detectable *physical* contribution cannot be reported yet: it
depends on a specified waveform, coupling to the spin Hamiltonian, and a
prespecified statistical test. Supplying any of those from C2/H01 now would be
a new hypothesis, which this reproduction gate explicitly forbids.

## Dominant conventional systematic to control

The magnetic-field projection in Eq. (1) is the dominant demonstrated
rotation-correlated effect. Its amplitude, phase, and readout contrast are
covariant in the short-time Figure 5 scan. Any future residual protocol must
measure the three-dimensional field orientation and rotation phase independently
and include field-alignment reversals/controls.

## Recommended ordinary-experiment improvements

- publish raw fluorescence ratios, timing records, and magnetometer data;
- sample more echo times and multiple rotation phases/rates;
- independently calibrate magnetic vector alignment and microwave pulse errors;
- pre-register a residual waveform and threshold before unblinding.

These are conventional experimental-design recommendations, not a claim that
an additional contribution exists.
